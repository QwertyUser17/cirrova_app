Cirrova privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Cirrova is a weather app. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting service. This policy describes everything the app sends off your phone and why.

What leaves your phone

Coordinates of your places, rounded to about 1 km. To get a forecast, air quality, warnings and river levels, the app sends the rounded latitude and longitude of each place directly to the weather service that answers for it:

ServiceWhat for
Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com)forecast, air quality and pollen, climate normals, city search
MET Norway (api.met.no)forecast for the Nordic countries, only while the app is open
MeteoAlarm (meteoalarm.org)official warnings in Europe
IMGW-PIB (imgw.pl)official warnings and river levels in Poland
National Weather Service (weather.gov)official warnings in the USA
Environment and Climate Change Canada (weather.gc.ca)official warnings and radar in Canada and the USA
NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov)readings of weather stations within about 40 km, for the weather now and the first hours of the forecast
NOAA Space Weather Prediction Centerthe northern lights (Kp) index; no location is sent

With "Detect location" on, the rounded position of your own place also goes to the location service built into Android (on most phones Google's) to get the name of the town.

City search sends the name you type. The radar and map screens load map, radar and satellite pictures for the area on screen from OpenStreetMap, ECCC, NASA GIBS and the Cirrova radar service; like any web request, these show the area you look at and your IP address to that server. The Cirrova radar server does not log requests for its pictures. It logs the IP address, time and app version of each request for its list of radar pictures, which the app makes when it shows the radar or checks for rain, and of requests the app never makes, to count visits and spot abuse. This log stays on the server, is shared with no one and is limited in size: the oldest entries are overwritten as new ones arrive. The router in front of the server may keep its usual short-lived traffic logs.

At each refresh the app also loads from the Cirrova radar service the one radar picture that covers each of your places, an area of roughly 100 km, to see whether anything is falling there now; like the pictures above, it shows that area and your IP address.

The app also asks the Cirrova website (qwertyuser17.github.io) where the radar service is, and the radar service which data sources are switched off; neither request carries anything about you. On the screen about background work, it asks dontkillmyapp.com for its advice for your phone's maker; the maker's name is all that request carries.

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy. Cirrova does not send them anything else: no name, no identifier of you or your device.

Fonts. If you pick a font that is not built into the app, Android downloads it from Google Fonts through Google Play services.

What stays on your phone

  • Your places, settings and widget settings.
  • Your approximate location, if you turn on "Detect location". It is read only while the app is open or the weather is being refreshed, and only its rounded coordinates are sent, as above.
  • Calendar: if you turn on "Next calendar event" in a widget, the widget reads the title and time of your next event from the phone's calendar to show it. Calendar data is never sent anywhere.
  • Photos you choose as backgrounds are copied into the app's own storage and never leave the phone; they are not part of the backup file.
  • The backup file you save is written where you choose and is not uploaded by the app.

Messages to the developer

"Report a problem" and "Report a translation error" open your email app with a message you write and send yourself. It includes the app version, the Android version, the device model and the app language, nothing else.

Rating request

Once, after you have used the app on several days, Google Play may show its own rating window. Cirrova does not see whether or how you rated it.

Children

Cirrova is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
