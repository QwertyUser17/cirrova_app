Warning areas from OpenStreetMap

The Cirrova app carries these outlines of administrative areas so that the phone itself can tell which official weather warnings cover a place. They are derived from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and are made available under the Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL).

Made from Geofabrik's OpenStreetMap extracts downloaded on 30 October 2026: the boundary relations tagged boundary=administrative, exported with osmium and simplified to about 500 m (0.005°). Each area keeps the codes a warning can name it by: NUTS codes and every shorter prefix of them (older NUTS codes from Wikidata, CC0), Polish TERYT county codes and MeteoAlarm's EMMA_ID codes that equal them, and DWD warn cells for German districts.

Each file is a JSON array of areas: k holds the keys as KIND:code, r the rings, outer rings and holes alike, each a flat list of longitude, latitude pairs. These are the files shipped with Cirrova 1.1.0.
